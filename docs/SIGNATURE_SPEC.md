# Acoustic Signature Specification — Transmedium Craft with Plasma-Sheath Propulsion

**Framing:** reported transmedium-UAP behavior is treated as a set of *hypotheses to model*, not established fact. This document derives the falsifiable, quantitative acoustic predictions those hypotheses entail, so that a search either finds the signature or places hard bounds on the hypothesis space. Produced by a four-lens analysis (plasma-sheath acoustics · transmedium transit acoustics · kinematic observables · confusion sources) consolidated under red-team review; companion document: [SEARCH_PLAN.md](SEARCH_PLAN.md).

## 2. Ranked discriminants

Measurements that separate this signature from every known source, ranked by uniqueness × measurability with verified public data:

### D1. Quiet-fast contradiction (speed-loudness impossibility)

- **Separates:** Separates a sheathed craft from EVERY known vehicle simultaneously: quiet submarines <45 kn, torpedoes ~55 kn (loud), supercavitating rockets ~200 kn at ~200+ dB for <3 min. A confirmed submerged speed >60 kn with broadband SL <170 dB (0.1-10 kHz) violates both U^6 dipole flow-noise scaling and cavitation-inception physics (U_i ~ 54 kn at 10 m depth) by >=20 dB — the margin absorbs the +/-10 dB anchor uncertainty. No open-literature drag-reduction technology closes that gap.
- **How to measure:** Coherent TDOA track on the LF network (|dtau/dt| up to 278 ms/s vs 5-ms timing gives huge per-window speed SNR) and/or single-phone Doppler swing v = c*(f+-f-)/(f++f-); sustained >=2 min; then an injection-calibrated broadband level UPPER BOUND at the fitted track range. Fires only with depth confirmation (excludes racing boats/hydrofoils/aircraft via Lloyd-mirror striations, 13.3-deg air-coupling cone, multipath ranging).
- **Data needed:** OOI 5-station 200-Hz GPS-timed LF network (TDOA validated to 25 km at 240 km); MARS 256 kHz or OOI 64 kHz calibrated broadband within range for the level bound; injection-measured detection floor per band.

### D2. Cross-band entry doublet with internal size consistency and catalog anti-coincidence

- **Separates:** Separates a genuine transmedium entry from airguns (fixed 10-15 s repetition), sparker shots (repetition + tow speed), lightning (global stroke catalogs), bolides (CNEOS/IMS infrasound), rocket/debris splashdowns (launch schedules, TIP messages), whale calls (tonal templates), and T-phases (20-200 s emergent envelopes). The doublet must be a SINGLETON whose slam-to-pinch delay t_p and Minnaert ring frequency return the SAME body/cavity scale.
- **How to measure:** Coincidence gate: HF impulse at MARS (0.1-30 kHz) and LF thump+doublet at OOI (3-100 Hz), travel-time corrected; extract t_p (0.1-0.5 s for 0.05-1 m bodies), infer a = g*(t_p/2)^2; extract ring f0, infer cavity volume; require agreement within a factor ~3; require zero repeats at fixed interval; require null joins against ALL external catalogs. The LF limb survives the biosonar-storm blindness that blinds the HF screen.
- **Data needed:** MARS full-coverage streaming (all 144 files/day — the 0.07% snippet screen cannot support singleton claims); OOI LF network; cross-site clock alignment <=0.1 s; AIS, GLD360/WWLLN, USGS/ISC, CNEOS + IMS infrasound, space-track TIP / NOTMAR feeds.

### D3. Coherent-Doppler composite spectrum (comb + hiss + LF lines locked to one track)

- **Separates:** Separates a single fast physical source from every stationary or slow interferer: MARS 50.04/100.08-kHz instrument tones (zero Doppler), 38-kHz echosounder (fixed carrier, clock-grade PRI), towed sparker surveys (2-3 m/s), vessels (<15 m/s, DEMON blade lines present), biologics (variable ICI, no harmonic comb), and multipath ghosts / source mixtures (components with unequal fractional shifts).
- **How to measure:** Track every narrowband feature with reassigned spectrograms (0.1-1 s windows so 4-300 Hz/s chirps do not smear); require identical fractional Doppler S-curve across all comb lines, the hiss envelope, and LF lines to <0.1%; require the CPA triple coincidence (level peak = Doppler inflection = max TDOA rate); require DEMON blade/shaft-line ABSENCE. Runs directly on the existing PRI/periodicity chain miner with its Poisson chance floor.
- **Data needed:** MARS 256 kHz 24-bit single station (sufficient); OOI LF for sub-100-Hz lines; existing PRI miner + DEMON demodulator; interferer exclusion list.

### D4. Post-doublet impulsive quench tail (hot-body flag)

- **Separates:** Separates a hot/sheathed body from every cold entry — debris splashdowns, breaching whales, dropped objects — which are acoustically silent after pinch-off. An irregular 1-100 Hz broadband (100 Hz-50 kHz) impulse train persisting 10-1000 s and bound to the entry position/track has no cold-physics or biological counterpart with that band-duration-irregularity conjunction.
- **How to measure:** After any candidate doublet, open a quench-time window and test for an impulsive tail: high kurtosis, irregular inter-pulse intervals (rejects the metronomic 0.4-0.5 Hz echosounder), broadband-to-LF content (rejects 15-50 kHz biosonar), no ICI terminal-buzz convergence, level track consistent with a slowly sinking/moving source at the doublet position.
- **Data needed:** MARS 256 kHz continuous full-coverage; transient chain with inter-event-interval statistics; multi-sensor bearing/TDOA if in mutual coverage (desirable, not required).

### D5. Interface-crossing pulse-order asymmetry (track topology)

- **Separates:** Separates from the closest kinematic mimic — torpedoes/supercavitating vehicles — whose tracks originate (launch transient near a platform) and terminate (shutdown/terminal collapse) WITHIN the water column and never cross the surface. Entry = impulse-first sequence at track start; exit = ramp-then-impulse-last at track end, optionally paired with an airborne counterpart (propagating boom or infrasound).
- **How to measure:** Fit track endpoints from sequential TDOA fixes; test whether the track begins at an entry doublet or ends at a void-collapse + 10-60 s plume ring; for full transits require the ordered conjunction entry-doublet -> run signature -> mirrored exit; check IMS/near-surface assets for the airborne limb.
- **Data needed:** OOI LF TDOA; MARS for the HF limbs; near-surface assets (Orcasound, shallow SanctSound CI01-05) for boom/breach airborne coupling; IMS infrasound catalogs.

### D6. Rayleigh-Willis / Minnaert depth self-consistency

- **Separates:** Separates a genuine discharge/boiling source AT DEPTH from towed near-surface sparkers, aliased instruments, and surface-vessel noise: bubble-ring frequency f1 = 1/(1.14*rho^(1/2)*E^(1/3)/P^(5/6)) and the Minnaert-weighted hiss peak must BOTH match the hydrostatic pressure at the independently measured (TDOA/multipath) depth for one consistent (E,P) pair. A 1-kJ pulse rings ~40 Hz near-surface vs ~1.7 kHz at 891 m — a 40x tag no mimic reproduces.
- **How to measure:** Per-pulse cepstrum on full-waveform data resolving 0.6-25 ms bubble periods; SL-derived pulse energy; depth from multipath/Lloyd-mirror structure or 3D TDOA; joint chi-square over (E,P); reject any candidate with no single-depth solution.
- **Data needed:** MARS 256 kHz waveform-level archive; multipath depth model for the site; ONC 512 kHz for >128-kHz collapse content (confirmation tier).

### D7. Supersonic trace velocity + mandatory energy-budget pairing

- **Separates:** Separates a hypersonic-in-air (>1482 m/s) approach or supersonic-in-water run from all acoustic sources — BUT the raw lens claim ('no acoustic source can exceed c in trace speed') is WRONG as stated: seismic P-wave arrivals from below sweep arrays faster than 1482 m/s routinely. The surviving discriminant is the conjunction: N-wave MORPHOLOGY + moving-point (hyperbolic, minutes-long) sweep matching a trajectory rather than a one-shot planar arrival + no seismic-catalog match + a subsequent entry event + the unavoidable energy deposit (drag power 10^11-10^12 W for supersonic-in-water — a continuous moving explosion, basin-detectable).
- **How to measure:** Array trace-speed fit >=1.05*c with N-wave waveform (50-300 ms, energy <50 Hz); trajectory (moving-source) vs plane-wave model comparison; USGS/ISC anti-join; require basin-scale LF/T-phase-class energy release for any supersonic-in-water claim — a supersonic track WITHOUT it is self-refuting.
- **Data needed:** OOI LF 5-station network (100+ km aperture, GPS timing; 200-Hz sampling adequate for <50-Hz N-wave energy); seismic catalogs; IMS hydroacoustic archive for the basin energy check.

### D8. Doppler step discontinuity (instantaneous-acceleration encoding)

- **Separates:** Separates hypothesized non-inertial maneuvering from every physical vehicle: a 10-g emergency maneuver produces a CONTINUOUS chirp of at most 0.66%/s of f0, never a step. Delta-f/f >= 1% completed in <= 0.1 s (>=15 g equivalent) with NO connecting chirp has no known-source explanation once frequency-agile sonar (step must appear on passively generated lines) and multipath ridge-jumping (multi-sensor coherence) are excluded.
- **How to measure:** Continuous ridge tracking of any narrowband line at 0.1-s resolution; require the identical fractional step simultaneously on >=2 unrelated spectral lines and/or >=2 sensors; exclude the 50.04/100.08-kHz instrument tones; model-compare step vs maximal-chirp fits.
- **Data needed:** MARS 256 kHz (high-SNR line required — this gun needs luck); second time-aligned sensor for coincidence; lowest measurability of the top eight, hence rank 8 despite high uniqueness.

### D9. Wake-without-noise (38-kHz bistatic backscatter line)

- **Separates:** Separates a sheath-generating but acoustically quiet craft from all known fast vehicles: anything leaving a bubble wake at speed is cavitating or propeller-driven and LOUD. A moving >=10-dB backscatter excess line with a QUIET passive record is positive evidence of an unconventional wake mechanism — and the only observable surviving a perfectly silent craft.
- **How to measure:** Ping-synchronous processing of the resident 38-kHz echosounder (0.4-0.5 Hz): >=5 consecutive pings with >=10 dB volume-backscatter excess forming a kinematically consistent translating line, coincident with no broadband elevation. Threshold is a screening choice, not derived — treat as confirmation-tier.
- **Data needed:** MARS 256 kHz during echosounder-active days only (~27% duty, ~100 d/yr); no new hardware; uncontrolled beam geometry limits coverage.

### D10. Catalog + statistical anti-coincidence gate (mandatory rejection filter)

- **Separates:** Not a positive discriminant but the gate every candidate must clear; it wholesale removes vessels/surveys (AIS), lightning (GLD360/WWLLN, ~1-5 km / <1 ms), earthquakes (USGS/ISC + T-phase travel time), bolides (CNEOS/IMS), splashdowns (launch manifests, TIP, NOTMAR), instruments (mooring asset lists, clock-grade PRI, fixed carriers), and biologics (sperm-whale 2-9 ms cepstral IPI, delphinid terminal-buzz ICI convergence, beaked-whale FM replica r>0.9, fin-whale downsweep template + winter seasonality, snapping-shrimp Poisson IEI + static bearing, fish diel choruses).
- **How to measure:** Automated joins with position/time gates (travel-time corrected); deterministic-replica matched-filter bank (>20 dB compression gain = known source); IEI/ICI statistics; DEMON blade-line detection; require at least TWO independent rejections to fail before a candidate escalates.
- **Data needed:** AIS feed, GLD360/WWLLN, USGS/ISC, CNEOS + IMS infrasound, space-track TIP/NOTMAR, OOI/ONC instrument metadata, template library; all public.

## 3. Kill criteria — observations that falsify a candidate

- KINEMATIC KILL: sequential TDOA fixes give track speed <=15 m/s matching an AIS vessel within the position/time gate, or a 2-3 m/s straight/lawn-mower line (sparker or chirp survey) — shot-to-shot fix spacing is the single decisive number against the sparker twin.
- REPETITION KILL: the 'entry impulse' repeats >=3 times at a stable 1-15 s interval with <1% jitter and >90% of energy below 150 Hz (airgun) or millisecond-jitter PRI for hours with a narrowband standard carrier at 38/70/120/200 kHz (echosounder/ADCP, cross-checked against mooring asset lists). Entry doublets must be singletons.
- DETERMINISM KILL: matched-filter compression gain >20 dB against a known LFM/FM replica (sub-bottom profiler, beaked-whale click r>0.9); stable 2-9 ms intra-click cepstral IPI (sperm whale); smooth terminal ICI convergence 100 ms -> <5 ms (delphinid buzz); stereotyped 23-18 Hz downsweep with winter seasonality (fin whale); >10-s constant/slow-FM tonals in series (blue whale) versus the predicted <1-2 s exponentially decaying cavity ring.
- DEMON KILL: envelope demodulation of the broadband continuum reveals shaft/blade-rate line structure (blade count x rpm/60) — propeller vessel, regardless of level or speed.
- CATALOG KILL: cataloged lightning stroke within 20 km and +/-2 s (travel-time corrected); USGS/ISC origin with predicted T-phase arrival within +/-30 s and back-azimuth within 10 deg; CNEOS/IMS-infrasound bolide match; launch-manifest/TIP/NOTMAR reentry ellipse match. One positive join kills the candidate.
- STATISTICAL-TEXTURE KILL: exponential (Poisson) inter-event intervals with static or <0.5 m/s drifting bearing (snapping shrimp beds, ice); dusk-onset diel or spawning-season scheduling (fish chorus).
- INTERNAL-CONSISTENCY KILL (single-source test): component fractional Doppler shifts differ beyond measurement error; or bubble-ring frequency has no Rayleigh-Willis (E,P) solution at the TDOA/multipath depth (e.g. ringing implies 10 m while TDOA says 800 m); or slam-to-pinch body scale and Minnaert cavity volume disagree by >~10x; or level peak, Doppler inflection, and max TDOA-rate are not simultaneous. Any one failure rejects the candidate as a real single moving source.
- ARTIFACT KILL: received levels imply SL >240-250 dB re 1 uPa @ 1 m at every propagation-consistent multi-station position, or the event appears on exactly one channel with no travel-time-consistent counterpart where coverage exists — near-field mooring artifact (strum, strike).
- QUIET-FAST RETRACTION: re-analysis finds >=180 dB broadband cavitation-band level on the fast track (consistent with a supercavitating vehicle — loud-fast is a NON-gun, possibly a classified test), or the speed solution revises below 60 kn, or depth confirmation fails (surface racer / aircraft coupling in the 13.3-deg cone with Lloyd-mirror striations).
- SUPERSONIC-CLAIM KILL: the >1482 m/s trace arrival fits a one-shot planar wavefront (seismic P from below) or associates with a seismic origin; or the claimed supersonic-in-water run shows NO basin-scale LF energy deposit — 10^11-10^12 W drag dissipation is unavoidable for any drag-producing body, so a quiet supersonic track is self-refuting and the supersonic interpretation must be dropped for that event.
- DOPPLER-STEP KILL: the step appears on one sensor only, appears only on active-sonar-like waveforms (frequency agility) and not on passively generated lines, or connects via a resolvable chirp consistent with <=10 g — conventional maneuver or waveform switch.
- QUENCH-TAIL KILL: the post-entry impulsive tail shows biosonar ICI dynamics, matches the 0.4-0.5 Hz echosounder clock, or does not remain spatially bound to the entry track.
- NULL-CLAIM VALIDITY: no null (including the no-splash bound a*u^2 < ~6 m^3/s^2) may be asserted unless the full event window (slam + t_p + quench time) lies inside recorded coverage and outside injection-verified blind periods (biosonar storms); a coverage gap yields NO claim in either direction.
- NO-SPLASH FALSIFICATION OF REPORTS: a corroborated (visual/radar) entry at u >> 4 m/s for a meter-scale body within ~10 km of MARS or in OOI LF range, with valid coverage and all three nulls (no slam, no irreducible LF thump, no doublet), falsifies the acoustic reality of the reported event as described — or quantifies a >20-40 dB (>99%) suppression anomaly (<~1 J radiated vs 10^2-10^4 J predicted); either way the anecdote is converted to a number.
- POPULATION KILL (hypothesis level): predicted source levels 190-240 dB re 1 uPa @ 1 m imply detection radii of tens-hundreds of km; N event-free days of full-coverage MARS+OOI screening therefore upper-bounds the (occurrence rate x duty cycle x power) product for MW-class sheath transits in the monitored volume — sustained non-detection progressively falsifies all variants of the hypothesis loud enough to matter.

## 4. Minimum sensor requirements per discriminant

## Appendix: lens analyses (full component tables)

### A. Plasma-sheath acoustics underwater

Lens 1 model: a plasma sheath sustained around a hull in seawater is acoustically a high-power electro-thermal source with five separable components. (1) If sustainment is pulsed, each pulse is a sparker-analog event: a microsecond shock (broadband, 100 Hz-100 kHz+) followed by vapor-bubble oscillation whose period obeys Rayleigh-Willis T = 1.14 rho^(1/2) E^(1/3) / P^(5/6) — at MARS depth (891 m, ~9.2 MPa) a 1 kJ pulse rings at ~1.7 kHz vs ~40 Hz near-surface, so depth is directly encoded in the bubble line. (2) The pulse train produces a spectral comb at the pulse-repetition frequency and harmonics — the single most searchable signature, directly matched to the existing PRI/periodicity chain-mining machinery, with average source level ~224 dB re 1 uPa @ 1 m for 10 MW electrical at 2% acoustic efficiency. (3) Continuous (RF/microwave) sustainment instead yields quasi-continuous boiling/condensation noise: at depth, vapor bubbles collapse in ~0.1 ms in subcooled water, giving impulsive, high-kurtosis hiss peaked 10-100+ kHz at overlap-rate so high it merges to a hiss; broadband SL ~191-241 dB re 1 uPa @ 1 m for 1 MW-1 GW dissipated at eta_ac 1e-4 to 1e-2. (4) The Leidenfrost vapor-film boundary sheds bubbles at Rayleigh-Taylor scales, amplitude-modulating the hiss at ~10-30 Hz and filling 20 Hz-40 kHz. (5) A drag-reduction supercavity radiates bell-shaped cavity-closure noise peaking 1-10 kHz (plus a 40-80 kHz natural-cavitation peak) scaling ~V^5-V^6, and low-frequency cavity-volume pulsation lines at ~1-30 Hz visible on the OOI 200 Hz TDOA network. Falsifiable discriminators vs known interferers: (a) PRF comb with per-pulse bubble ringing whose frequency fits Rayleigh-Willis for a single consistent depth-energy pair; (b) hiss with simultaneous LF pulsation lines and kHz-comb, all Doppler-shifted together (up to ~7% at 100 m/s, c=1490 m/s) with a coherent CPA sweep; (c) source levels 190-240 dB re 1 uPa @ 1 m imply detection at tens-hundreds of km, so absence of any such event in full-coverage MARS/OOI screening bounds the (power x duty x occurrence-rate) product of the hypothesis.

### B. Transmedium transit (entry/exit) acoustics

Lens 2 defines the acoustic signature family for transmedium (air-water) transit of a hypothetical plasma-sheathed craft, treating reported UAP behavior as hypotheses. A high-speed water ENTRY is predicted to be a stereotyped multi-pulse sequence: (1) a broadband water-hammer slam (peak pressures scaling as beta*rho*c*u, effective SL_pk ~230-260 dB re 1 uPa @ 1 m for meter-scale bodies at 50-300 m/s, us-ms duration), (2) an irreducible low-frequency volume-displacement 'thump' whose level depends only on size and speed (SL_pk = 20log10(rho*a*u^2/2); ~208 dB for a=0.5 m at 10 m/s; 5-20 Hz — inside the OOI 200-Hz LF band where our TDOA localization is validated), (3) a quiet open-cavity run, then (4) a deep-seal pinch-off pulse at t_p = (1.7-2.3)*sqrt(a/g) after impact (0.4-0.5 s for a 1-m body) with Minnaert ringing of the detached bubble at f = 3.26*sqrt(1+z/10)/R_b Hz (units m, m/s: ~3-30 Hz for meter-scale cavities), SL_pk 210-235 dB — the slam/pinch-off DOUBLET with size-diagnostic delay and frequency is the primary template. A HOT or plasma-sheathed body inverts naive expectations: a stable Leidenfrost/vapor jacket produces a smooth streamlined cavity with up to 85% drag reduction and SUPPRESSED splash/contact noise, but adds signatures a cold body cannot produce — sustained film-boiling wake noise (broadband 0.1-20 kHz plus 10-100 Hz bubble-cloud collective oscillation, lasting the quench time ~10-1000 s), and vapor-explosion (film-collapse) chugging: sub-ms pressure pulses at 200-230 dB SL_pk repeating at 1-100 Hz as the surface cools through the Leidenfrost point — a post-entry impulsive tail that is the sharpest hot-vs-cold discriminator. A maintained underwater electrical discharge is acoustically a sparker: 200-230 dB re 1 uPa @ 1 m per kJ-scale pulse, spectral peak migrating from ~1 kHz (10 J) to ~20 Hz (>4 kJ). EXIT is predicted to be the time-reverse asymmetry test: gradual noise onset, then a void-refill collapse pulse (195-220 dB, few-30 Hz) and a 10-60 s bubble-plume ring (50-500 Hz, 160-185 dB) with NO preceding slam — pulse ordering separates entry from exit. Sonic-boom coupling gives a clean speed discriminator: for airspeed < 1482 m/s (M<4.3) the boom is evanescent (e-fold depth v/(2*pi*f*sqrt(1-(v/c_w)^2)), ~5-15 m at 10 Hz; F-4 field data show booms vanish into noise by 30-50 m depth, >20 Hz components gone below ~10 m) — a hard NULL at MARS (891 m) and OOI seafloor depths; for airspeed > 1482 m/s the boom couples as a real propagating wave (underwater peak ~2x airborne overpressure, 166-186 dB, N-wave 50-300 ms, energy <50 Hz, ~-30 dB energy transmission), SOFAR-trackable at 100+ km with trace speed >1482 m/s across the LF array as an unambiguous hypersonic-in-air marker; subsonic airborne noise reaches deep hydrophones only within the ~13.3-deg critical-angle cone (near-overhead footprint radius ~0.24x altitude). The NO-SPLASH hypothesis is made falsifiable via the volume-flux floor: non-detection at MARS/OOI within 10 km bounds SL_pk < ~190 dB, which via component (2) forces a*u^2 < ~6 m^3/s^2 — i.e., a meter-scale body must cross slower than ~3.5-4 m/s, and any corroborated surface event with faster entry that produces no slam, no pinch-off doublet, and no LF thump implies >20-40 dB (>99%) acoustic suppression relative to rigid-body hydrodynamics, quantified as radiated energy < ~1 J vs 10^2-10^4 J predicted (eta ~ 1e-4-1e-3 of kinetic energy). Search-design consequence: entry events are jointly detectable as an HF impulse at MARS (256 kHz) and an LF doublet/thump on the OOI 200-Hz network — a cross-band, cross-site coincidence test with TDOA localization is the highest-value screen, and the LF band survives the biosonar-storm blindness that limits the HF screen.

### C. Kinematic / motion observables

Lens 3 derives the kinematic/motion observables that a moving underwater source at claimed UAP speeds (50-400 kn, with hypothesized instantaneous acceleration) would imprint on single hydrophones (MARS 256 kHz) and arrays (OOI LF network, validated TDOA). Core results: (1) Doppler is the single-phone speed meter — fractional shift ~v/c gives +1.77/-1.71% at 50 kn, +3.60/-3.36% at 100 kn, +16.2/-12.2% at 400 kn on any tone or pulse-repetition frequency, with a characteristic S-curve through CPA whose total swing yields v and whose CPA slope yields range (v = c*(f+ - f-)/(f+ + f-); R_cpa = f0*v^2/(c*|df/dt|_CPA)). (2) The level-time record is a symmetric hyperbola RL(t) = SL - 20log10(sqrt(R_cpa^2 + v^2 t^2)) with -3 dB half-width R_cpa/v (2.4 s at 400 kn for R_cpa = 500 m) — a fast close transit is a seconds-scale event, confirming that coverage, not sensitivity, dominates search design. (3) Hydrodynamic noise physics makes 'fast AND quiet' the primary smoking gun: cavitation inception is unavoidable above U_i(d) = sqrt(2(p_inf(d) - p_v)/(rho*sigma_i)) — roughly 54 kn at 10 m, 128 kn at 100 m, 366 kn even at MARS's 891 m — and wetted-flow dipole noise scales as U^6 (+18 dB per speed doubling), so any known-physics craft at 100+ kn shallow must radiate ~180-210 dB re 1 uPa @ 1 m broadband (supercavitating rocket torpedoes, the only >60 kn precedent, are ~200 dB and last minutes). (4) Supersonic-in-water motion (>1480 m/s) would produce a trailing Mach-cone N-wave with no acoustic precursor, along-array trace velocity >1480 m/s, and a drag-power budget of order 10^11-10^12 W — a continuous moving explosion detectable basin-wide; a supersonic TDOA track WITHOUT that energy release is internally inconsistent under known physics and is therefore the strongest possible anomaly. (5) Arrays add TDOA-rate (|dtau/dt| up to 2v/c = 278 ms/s at 400 kn — trivially measurable at OOI's 5-ms timing) and multi-station track speed. The falsifiable smoking-gun set: (A) sustained TDOA/Doppler-confirmed submerged speed >60 kn for >2 min with broadband level <170 dB (quiet-fast contradiction); (B) step Doppler discontinuity Delta-f/f > 1% in <0.1 s on a tracked tone/PRF (>15 g radial jerk, no inertial vehicle); (C) speed >2x U_i(depth) with no cavitation spectrum but WITH a bubbly/ionized wake visible as a moving 38 kHz backscatter line (the MARS echosounder becomes an asset); (D) N-wave with supersonic trace velocity. Each has a stated known-source ceiling: fastest quiet submarine <45 kn, fastest torpedo ~55 kn (loud), supercavitating rocket ~200 kn (extremely loud, short-lived), fast surface craft and low aircraft are excluded via Lloyd-mirror/critical-angle depth discrimination.

### D. Confusion sources and their rejecting measurements

Confusion matrix for a hypothesized plasma-sheath transmedium craft signature against 20 known ocean source classes. The hypothesized signature has five mimicable elements: (E1) water-entry impact impulse plus bubble-cavity oscillation (broadband crack then tens-to-hundreds-Hz Minnaert ringing); (E2) sustained broadband electrical-discharge 'crackle' continuum (sparker-like, ~100 Hz-50 kHz); (E3) a fast-moving broadband source with large Doppler/TDOA-derived speed (>30-100 m/s); (E4) possible pulsed-discharge PRI trains; (E5) cavitation-like continuum from supercavitating underwater transit. The single most dangerous confusers are: sparker seismic surveys (genuine underwater electrical plasma discharges, spectrally near-identical to E2), rocket-stage/debris splashdowns (genuine transmedium impacts, near-identical to E1), and supercavitating vehicles (kinematically closest to E3/E5). Every mimic is rejectable with one targeted measurement, and the rejections fall into four reusable classes: (a) waveform/template determinism (echosounders, ADCPs, chirp profilers, whale call templates, click multipulse structure); (b) kinematics from multi-sensor TDOA (animal speeds <10 m/s, vessels <15 m/s, survey lines at 2-3 m/s, stationary shrimp beds; a transmedium craft is hypothesized >30 m/s with an interface-crossing discontinuity); (c) external-catalog coincidence (AIS for vessels/surveys, GLD360/WWLLN for lightning, USGS/ISC for T-phases, CNEOS/infrasound for bolides, launch schedules/NOTMARs/space-track TIP for splashdowns, seasonal presence for baleen whales); (d) statistical texture (Poisson inter-event intervals for shrimp and ice, diel/seasonal choruses for fish, ICI dynamics for biosonar). The decisive positive discriminant left after all rejections: a single event exhibiting E1 followed by E2/E3 with a coherent supersonic-to-fast TDOA track that crosses the air-water interface, with NO match in any of catalogs (a)-(c) - no known source class produces that conjunction.

**Assumptions:** The plasma-sheath transmedium signature is HYPOTHESIZED, not established: elements assumed are (E1) water-entry impulse + Minnaert cavity ringing (f ~ 3.26/R_bubble Hz-m, i.e. tens-hundreds Hz for 0.03-1 m cavities), (E2) sparker-like broadband discharge continuum ~100 Hz-50 kHz, (E3) TDOA/Doppler track speed >30 m/s (reported-UAP kinematics taken as hypothesis), (E4) possible stable pulsed-discharge PRI, (E5) supercavitation-like continuum if submerged transit is fast. · Source levels quoted mix peak-to-peak and rms conventions as in the source literature (pp for impulsive biosonar/sparker/airgun, rms for tonal/continuous); all dB re 1 uPa @ 1 m; comparisons across conventions carry ~9-15 dB ambiguity. · Rejection tests assume access to: multi-station TDOA (validated on OOI LF array to ~25 km at 240 km range), AIS feeds, global lightning networks (GLD360/WWLLN), USGS/ISC seismic catalogs, CNEOS/IMS infrasound bolide catalogs, and launch/reentry schedules (NOTMAR, space-track TIP); where only a single hydrophone is available (MARS), the kinematic rejections degrade to Doppler + received-level plausibility. · Band and PRI figures are site-relevant to MARS (256 kHz) and OOI (200 Hz / 64 kHz); sources above 128 kHz Nyquist at MARS alias and were treated at their aliased appearance where relevant. · Military torpedo/supercavitating-vehicle source levels are estimates from open literature and scaling, not measured public data; the ~110 m/s Shkval speed ceiling is from public reporting. · Lightning underwater coupling levels follow Hill (1985)-type modeling and are the least-constrained numbers in the matrix. · Each 'derivation' field names the single best rejecting measurement; in practice adjudication should require at least two independent rejections before escalating a candidate.
